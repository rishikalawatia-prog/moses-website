“Find the right people, build the right partnerships, and grow the right business.”

With over 27 years of experience, I have built my career around one core strength — identifying the right talent for the right business, and building the right partnerships to grow it.

I started my journey in 1998 in the Recruitment & Staffing business, partnering with some of India's top technology companies. In staffing, I specialised in identifying high-quality resources and placing them on a one-time fee model, along with end-to-end staffing services — and have executed numerous onsite placements of technology resources across the world.

While staffing remained my core, I expanded into entrepreneurship in Retail, then into Aerospace as a reseller of pressure transducers to DRDO Labs, and founded iTecno Labs, an incubation centre for product development companies.

In the last few years, my focus has been on helping companies acquire, merge and build meaningful partnerships — bridging gaps, enabling collaboration, and creating value through strategic Mergers & Acquisitions.
